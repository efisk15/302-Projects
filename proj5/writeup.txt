Questions:
What is complexity of your implementation?

A:
Edmonds-Karp time complexity = O(V * E^2).

Vertexes = Dice + Letters + 2.
Edges = O(Dice * Letters).

Simplifies to O(Dice * Letters ^ 2 + L ^ 3).

This is because my implementation uses a vector of node structs and edges between them.
